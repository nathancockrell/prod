people feel motivated to achieve a desired state of life, a "fantasy"
    desire to be eating dinner, or have eaten dinner>motivated to make dinner
the method to achieve this will naturally gravitate toward being the least painful and quickest one.
### A desired state of life is one with different current statistics
- I have 0 enemies.
- I have 0 fatigue.
- I have 2 cookies.
or
- I have 0 hunger.

However, there exists a fundamental desire for meaning which is really just an emotional bottom limiter on life.
This desire for meaning alters the method we take to achieve our desired states.
### A desired meaning is a history of different statistics
- I did this one thing a lot. so I am very comfortable doing this thing and by transitive property more comfortable doing all things especially those easier than this thing
- AKA: I have done 20,000 pushups (note:the statistic isn't I look healthy or I am healthy, those are "current" statistics)
- I helped a lot of people/made a lot of people happy or made their lives better.
- AKA: I have mowed 1,000 lawns (the statistic is not that you have $15,000)

# We desire both.
- with morals, we think and care a lot about the way we do things.
- this greatly affects our generated desired outcomes.

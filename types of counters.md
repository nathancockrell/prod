#### types of statistics you want to track, "counters"

## Primary
- a defined action/event
- total amount of time doing action/event
- total amount of time (doing actions)toward achieving desired outcome
- number of days a pre-established action or amount time spent toward something was fulfilled

## ancillary
- streak, days in a row the number of days was increased
- downtime, time spent processing or recovering

#### in addition to types, there is also the period during which you want to track them
- number of actions (I'll track this until I've done it 3 times)
- amount of time spent (I'll track this until i spend 50 hours doing it)
- exterior time period (for 10 days i'll track this)
